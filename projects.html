ios-arch-kit

Architecture guardrails for agentic coding

A portable kit that encodes iOS architectural conventions as machine-readable constraints an agent reads before writing code. Conventions were extracted from a production reference codebase across six structured passes under a hard evidentiary rule: every claim cites a real file path, nothing generalised from a single occurrence, no invented principles. Claims are tagged evidenced or extrapolated, and the ratio is tracked as a quality metric.

Ships as an always-on specification under 60 lines, an architecture skill with a reference directory, an installer, and documentation of the extraction passes.

How I know it works

I built the portable version of this as ios-arch-kit and evaluated it blind, guided against unguided.

The scorecard matters as much as the headline number. I ran the kit's architectural predictions against a real async/await migration in the reference repository, then turned the hit rate into a calibration rule applied to the remaining claims. Predicting where you will be wrong, and pricing it, is the part that generalises.

When it goes wrong

During the extraction I caught a fabricated statistic that had already propagated through three hops of generated files. I logged it as evidence for mandatory human review gates. I also caught my own error: I had briefed the agents with the wrong baseline commit, twice. Two agents flagged it, and the real baseline was established by CI-workflow blob match plus file modification times.

Both incidents are in the record on purpose. “How do you handle AI reliability?” deserves a real answer with a root cause and a process change, not a reassurance.

A defended position. Storage ports are synchronous throws; network ports are async throws. A store protocol never says async, never says @MainActor, and never names a queue — asynchrony is injected above it, and the composition root absorbs the churn. Verified against the repository's real concurrency migration: the seam modelling a domain capability survived; the seam modelling a concurrency mechanism was deleted by the new mechanism.
